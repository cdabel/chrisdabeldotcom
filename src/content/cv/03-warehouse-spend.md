Across Snowflake, dbt Cloud, and tooling, with no loss of reliability. The lever was not tuning. Ninety-two tenant-flattening models were rebuilt nightly when nine fed anything downstream, and each one fanned out across about 120 tenant schemas. Disabling the rest took pipeline query volume from 13 million a month to under 2 million, an 86% drop that tracks a single commit. Deleting work beat optimizing it by an order of magnitude.
