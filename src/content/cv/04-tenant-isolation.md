Snowflake governance from scratch for the company's LLM surfaces: Row Access Policies over an entitlements table, least-privilege service-account roles, and role-based masking on free text and financial values, so each surface sees only what its tenant is entitled to. Then the unglamorous half: every service account moved onto Snowflake's actual service-user type, keys rotated, and SCIM 2.0 discovery work alongside the IT security team. None of it is exotic. All of it is what an auditor asks about first.
